# Gold Star

## Elevator Pitch
Millions of people struggle with ADHD symptoms, such as, starting tasks, staying focused, and actually finishing what they begin. While building routines is one of the most effective solutions, it's also one of the hardest things to do when consistency itself is the challenge. Gold Star is a web-based habit building and time management platform designed specifically for individuals with ADHD. It helps users organize their schedules, break down tasks and build sustainable routines which make it easier to start, stay on track, and follow through everyday.

## What is the Problem?
Individuals diagnosed with ADHD or demonstrating ADHD-like traits struggle with building consistent routines. ADHD involves certain productivity-altering traits such as "inattention, hyperactivity and impulsivity" (Staley et al., 2024, p. 890). Adults with ADHD are also more likely to have “memory problems, restlessness and lack of mental focus” (Advanced Psychiatry Associates, 2026). Simultaneously, ADHD adults may experience excessive focus on specific tasks or hyper-focusing (Chen et al., 2026, p. 2). Given that an estimated 8% of adults in the United States experience ADHD as a lifelong condition and research suggests an estimated “4-5% lower on-the-job work performance than other workers”, routine development is expected to be consistently frustrating (Advanced Psychiatry Associates, 2026; Kessler et al., 2008, p. 141). In an ADHD adult’s experience, routines may last anywhere between three days to “part of a month”, but may linger outside the range until an abrupt schedule change (ADHD Philadelphia, 2026). Routines are prone to change, routine changes lead to increased activity switching. When activity switching independently, ADHD adults reportedly experienced “heightened frustration or anxiety in response to task demands, reinforcing cycles of avoidance and reduced self-efficacy” (Chen et al., 2026, p. 2). Avoidance leads to tasks and responsibilities left behind while relationships strain due to misunderstandings of an ADHD individual’s needs (Chen et al., 2026, p. 7). For people with ADHD, time management is a harrowing ordeal.

## Our Solution 
Gold Star is a web-based routine management platform designed to help individuals with ADHD organize their time, tasks, and daily reponsibilities. It provides a flexible system that adapts to changing schedules and missed tasks while helping users build sustainable habits with structure and accountability. Through an intuitive, calendar-based interface, users can plan, track, and maintain routines without feeling overwhelmed. By combining flexibility with habit-building strategies, Gold star helps users stay consistent, complete tasks, and better manage their daily lives. 

## Target Customers and End Users
The intended primary consumer demographic of this software would be individuals with ADHD who struggle with time management, organization, and maintaining routines in their daily lives. Many people with ADHD face challenges when creating and sustaining consistent schedules due to difficulties with focus, motivation, and prioritizing tasks efficiently overall. Given the software’s purpose to automate building, maintaining, and adapting end user routines, the customer would serve as Gold Star’s primary end user role intending to use the application to improve productivity and schedule balancing.

Although individuals with ADHD are the main focus of this software, it could also benefit other groups. Additional end user demographics may include parents, guardians, therapists, and other neurodivergent or neurotypical people seeking assistance with time management and routine building skills. Although Gold Star was developed for individuals with ADHD, the application is not intended to exclude people without ADHD. Regardless of symptoms, people who struggle with organization, productivity, or habit-building consistency are expected to find this application useful for managing daily tasks and stable routines.
